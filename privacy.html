2. Personal data we collect

2.1 Contact form

When you send an enquiry through the “Get in Touch” form, we collect: your name, email address, hotel or company name (optional), the topic of your enquiry (optional) and your message.

2.2 Cloudbeds demo request form

When you request a Cloudbeds introduction, we collect:

  • Property details: property name, website, business type, property type, number of rooms and a brief description of the property;
  • Property address: country, city/province, state/province (optional) and postal code (optional);
  • Contact details: first name, last name, email address, phone number and what prompted your interest in Cloudbeds.

2.3 Direct contact

If you email or call us directly, we process the information you choose to share with us, such as your name, contact details and the content of your communication.

2.4 Technical data

When you visit the website, our hosting provider automatically processes technical information that your browser sends, such as your IP address, the date and time of the request, the page requested, browser type and operating system. This is necessary to deliver the website securely. We do not use this data to identify you.

Please do not include sensitive personal data (for example health information) in your messages.

4. Cloudbeds introductions

TAOVEL is a Cloudbeds Ambassador and may receive a commission when a hotel subscribes to Cloudbeds through its referral. If you submit a demo request, we review your details and, with your consent, share the information necessary for the introduction (such as your property details and contact details) with Cloudbeds so that Cloudbeds can contact you and provide a demonstration. Once shared, Cloudbeds processes your data as an independent controller under its own privacy policy, available on the Cloudbeds website.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

5. Service providers and recipients

We use carefully selected service providers who process personal data on our behalf, only as needed to provide their services:

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) — receives the data you enter in our forms and forwards it to us by email.
  • Google (Gmail) — email hosting for our business correspondence.
  • GitHub (GitHub Pages) — website hosting; processes technical data when you visit the site.

We may also share personal data with professional advisers (such as accountants or legal advisers) where necessary, or with public authorities where we are legally required to do so. Cloudbeds receives data only as described in section 4.

6. International data transfers

Some of our service providers are located in, or may process data in, countries outside the European Economic Area, including the United States. Where this is the case, we rely on appropriate safeguards provided by the GDPR, such as an adequacy decision (including the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified) or the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses. You can contact us to obtain more information about these safeguards.

7. How long we keep your data

  • Enquiries and demo requests that do not lead to a business relationship are kept for up to 24 months after our last contact, then deleted.
  • Business correspondence with clients is kept for as long as necessary for the relationship and for the retention periods required by tax and commercial law.
  • Technical data is kept by our hosting provider for a limited period for security purposes.

You may ask us to delete your data earlier — see section 8.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • rectify inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • erase your data (“right to be forgotten”);
  • restrict processing in certain circumstances;
  • data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format;
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interests;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent.

To exercise your rights, email us at tuncer.a.oguz@gmail.com. We will respond within one month, as required by the GDPR.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU Member State of your habitual residence, place of work or place of the alleged infringement. In Greece, this is the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (www.dpa.gr).

9. Cookies and tracking

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers or social media plugins. If this changes, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent first.

10. Security

The website is served over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against loss, misuse and unauthorised access. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Children

This website is intended for hospitality businesses and professionals. It is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when our services or legal requirements change. The current version is always available on this page, with the date of the latest update shown at the top.
