FAQ

Questions hotel owners often ask.

What does a hospitality consultant do for a boutique hotel?

A hospitality consultant looks at the hotel as a whole — concept, guest experience, daily operations and the systems behind them — and turns that into clear, prioritised improvements the owner and team can act on.

Do you work with small and independent hotels?

Yes. TAOVEL focuses on independent and boutique hotels, guesthouses and small resorts that want senior advice without the overhead of a large consulting firm.

Where do you work?

TAOVEL is based in Greece and works with independent and boutique hotels in every European country, from Greek islands to city hotels across Europe. Most of the work is done remotely through online meetings, shared documents and access to your systems.

Can you help us choose or switch to a new PMS such as Cloudbeds?

Yes. We help you assess your current setup, choose suitable technology and plan the move, including Cloudbeds PMS, channel manager and booking engine. TAOVEL is a Cloudbeds Ambassador and may receive a commission when a hotel subscribes through its referral.

How does a project start?

With a short introductory call to understand your property and goals. After that you receive a written proposal with a clear scope, timeline and fixed price.
